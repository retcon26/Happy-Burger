HAPPY BURGER - HOW TO PLAY  (v0.1.0)
=========================================

Start Shift on the title screen, then pick how hard: Tutorial (a guided
first burger), Easy, Medium, Hard or Extreme Burgers. The kitchen is behind the counter with its
three yellow trays; along the back wall: the fridge, the freezer, the grill,
the buns, the topping bins, a cutting board, the fryer. Another board, the
soda machine and the bin are at the sides.

CONTROLS                     PSP
---------------------------  -------------------------
Walk                         Analog nub
Look                         Square / Circle (left / right),
                               Triangle / Cross (up / down)
Grab                         R: on the fridge, buns, bins,
                               freezer, fryer or soda machine
                               it takes one; on food it picks
                               it up; holding food, it puts
                               it down
Throw                        L (while holding food)
Jump                         L (hands empty)
Crouch                       Tap D-pad Down
Quick chat (co-op)           Hold D-pad Down
Talk (co-op, with a mic)     Hold D-pad Up
Staff list                   Hold Select
Pause                        Start

D-pad Right does what R does on stations, and holding it on food carries it.
Look at anything and the bottom of the screen says what it is.

COOKING
-------
Patties and sausages cook on the grill: pink (raw), brown (cooked!),
dark (overdone, half pay), black (burnt).

Build a burger on a cutting board: a bottom bun, the toppings on the
ticket (C cheese, L lettuce, T tomato), the cooked patty, then a top bun.
A hot dog is a cooked sausage and a hot dog bun on a board. Fries come
out of the fryer, sodas out of the machine. Put each meal on the yellow
tray in front of the customer who ordered it. Drop mistakes in the bin.

Hit a customer with flying food and they storm off. Run out of stars and
the health inspector closes you down. Make the day's $ quota to keep
your job.
