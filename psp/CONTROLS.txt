RETCON'S MOD (FORMERLY PSP SANDBOX) - CONTROLS  (v1.11.0)
=========================================

ON FOOT                      PSP
---------------------------  -------------------------
Move                         Analog nub
Look                         Square / Circle (left /
                             right), Triangle / Cross
                             (up / down)
Jump                         L
Crouch (toggle)              Tap D-pad Down
Quick chat (messages and     Hold D-pad Down
  gestures; see below)
Fire / use weapon            R
Reload                       Hold D-pad Left
Next weapon                  Tap D-pad Left
Use (doors, buttons,         D-pad Right
  thrusters, hoverballs)
Carry a light prop           Hold D-pad Right
  (let go to drop, R throws)
Flashlight                   Hold D-pad Right
Undo last prop/NPC/ragdoll   D-pad Up (multiplayer:
                               a quick tap)
Talk (multiplayer, with a    Hold D-pad Up
  mic: the PSP-3000's
  built-in one, or a headset)
Spawn menu                   Tap Select
Noclip (fly)                 Hold Select
  - in noclip: Jump = up, hold D-pad Down = down
Pause menu                   Start
Respawn after dying          X or R


WEAPONS (D-pad Left cycles through them)
  Hands, Physics Gun, Gravity Gun, Tool Gun, Crowbar, Pistol, .357 Magnum, SMG, Shotgun,
  AR2, Crossbow, RPG, Grenade, Bug Bait

Physics Gun
  Grab and hold              Hold R
  Freeze in place            L (while holding)
  Push away / pull closer    D-pad Up / Down
  Rotate                     D-pad Left / Right
  Rotate on every axis       Hold X and push the circle pad (left/right
                               turns it, up/down tips it away or towards
                               you). You stand still while you do.
  Works on props and on ragdolls (grab any limb).

Gravity Gun
  Pick up / drop             R
  Launch (punt)              L
  Lifts up to 250 kg from a distance.

Hands
  Pick up / drop             R
  Throw                      L
  Close, light things only.

AR2                          R fires (hold for automatic)
Crossbow                     R fires a bolt: fast, drops a little, sticks in walls.
                             Reloads after every shot.
RPG                          R fires a rocket. It steers towards wherever you aim
                             until it hits, so keep aiming at the target.
Grenade                      R throws one. It bounces and goes off after 2.5 seconds.
Bug Bait                     Tap R: throw it. Antlions swarm where it lands.
                             Hold R: squeeze it. Antlions come to you and follow you.
                             After you've used it, antlions are on your side for the
                             rest of the map (headcrabs and antlion guards aren't).

Tool Gun
  Pick the tool in the spawn menu's Tools panel, then press R.
  The top-left box shows what the tool does and what to press next.
  Tools: Remover, Colour, Balloon, Duplicator, Weld, Rope, Ball Socket, Elastic,
         No-Collide, Weight, Thruster, Hoverball, Explosive, Slider,
         Inflator, Deflator (resize ragdoll body parts), Camera, Wheel,
         Winch, Hydraulic, Lamp, Light, Button, Emitter (L: change effect),
         Face Poser, Finger Poser (L: change expression / hand pose)
  Use (D-pad Right) on a prop switches its thrusters, hoverballs, wheel
  motors, winches, hydraulics, lamps, lights and emitters. Use on a button
  switches the whole contraption it was linked to.

Face Poser and Finger Poser (Tool Gun)
  Pose the ragdoll you aim at R
  Next expression / pose     L (the top-left box shows which)
  Face Poser: Neutral, Smile, Grin, Angry, Sad, Surprised, Scared, Pain,
    Shout, Eyes Shut. Neutral puts the ragdoll's own face back.
  Finger Poser: Natural, Fist, Point, Thumbs Up, Peace, Rock On, Open, OK.
    Aim at a hand to pose that hand, or at the body to pose both.

Camera tool (Tool Gun)
  Place a camera             R
  Look through the next      L
    camera (after the last
    one, back to your eyes)
  A camera faces the way you were looking when you placed it, and stays put.
  Move or weld it like any prop (Physics Gun), e.g. onto a car.
  Putting the tool away returns to your own view. L doesn't jump while the
  Camera tool is out.


ITEMS (spawn menu > Items)
  Health Kit, Health Vial, Suit Battery: walk over one to take it (only when
    it would help: health under 100, or suit power under 100 with the HEV suit on).
  Ammo (Pistol, .357, SMG, Shotgun Shells, AR2, Crossbow Bolts, RPG Round,
    Grenade): walk over it to take it, when that gun isn't full.
  Health Charger, Suit Charger: tap Use (D-pad Right) on one to charge; it stops
    when you're full, it's empty, you walk away or you tap Use again. They
    fill up again slowly. Move them onto a wall with the Physics Gun.


VEHICLES AND SEATS
  Spawn the Jeep, Airboat or a seat from the spawn menu's Vehicles category.
  Get in / out               Use (D-pad Right), aiming at it from close by
  Drive                      Analog nub: up / down is throttle and brake (and reverse),
                             left / right steers
  Handbrake                  Hold L (the Jeep's back end slides out)
  Seats: weld, rope or hinge one to your contraption, sit in it, and press R to switch
  every thruster and hoverball on the contraption on and off.
  Look around with the face buttons; Third-person view (Options) follows from behind.


SPAWN MENU (tap Select)

  L / R               Switch between Categories, Items and Tools panels
  D-pad               Move around a panel (Right/Left also steps between panels)
  X                   Spawn the item / pick the tool
  Circle or Select    Close
  Start               Close and pause
  Spawnlists: Recent (what you last spawned), your lists, NPCs and Ragdolls, then one
  header per game (+). On a game: X or Right opens its lists (closing the open one),
  Left closes it again.
  A wheel badge marks props you can drive or sit in.



QUICK CHAT (hold D-pad Down)
  A wheel of messages (Hello!, Thanks!, Yes, No, Follow me!, Wait here, Help!,
  Look at this!) and gestures (Wave, Salute, Yes, No, Come here, Cheer, Laugh,
  Dance). Push the circle pad towards one, L / R turns the page, let go of
  Down to send. Messages go in everyone's chat; everyone sees your gestures
  (and you do in the third-person view). Walking off stops a gesture.


PAUSE MENU (Start)
  Resume Game, Options, Saves, Screenshot, Toggle Noclip, Freeze All Props,
  Clean Up Props (also clears blood), Disconnect
  Screenshot saves the screen (without this menu) as a PNG in PSP/PHOTO/
  RetconsMod, where the XMB's Photo app finds it.
  D-pad Up / Down to choose, X to select, Circle or Start to resume.


PAUSE MENU > SAVES (single player and hosting)
  Saves everything you've spawned (props, welds, ropes and the rest,
  thrusters, hoverballs, balloons, lamps, lights, buttons and emitters) to
  data/saves on the memory stick, and loads it back on any map.
  "Save the current build": X names it with the on-screen keyboard.
  On a save:  X        load it where you're looking, turned the way you face
              Square   load it where it was built (on the map it was built on)
              Triangle delete it (press twice)
  One Undo takes a whole loaded build away.


OPTIONS
  D-pad Up / Down to choose, X or Right / Left to change, Circle to go back.
  Includes: Sound, Look speed, Invert look, God mode, Player model,
  Third-person view, NPC thinking, NPCs ignore players, Unlimited ammo,
  HEV suit, Show HUD, Crosshair, Show FPS, Target FPS, Draw distance,
  LOD distance, Physics quality, Updates, Credits.
  Target FPS: 25, 30, 60 (default) or 120. The screen shows 60 at most; 120
  doesn't wait for it, so the picture can tear.
  LOD distance: a slider, 0.5x to 2x, for how far props and NPCs are drawn
  before they pop out (default 1x, the middle).
  Updates (main menu only): checks GitHub over Wi-Fi and installs a newer
  version of the game. X installs, then quit and start the game again. The
  old version stays as EBOOT.OLD in the game's folder.


MULTIPLAYER
  Host Game / Join Game     ad-hoc, PSP to PSP
  Host on Wi-Fi             host through your access point: PSPs on the
                            same Wi-Fi find it in Online Servers
  Online Servers            games on your Wi-Fi and dedicated servers
                            (retcons-mod-server). Triangle adds a server's
                            address (a name or 1.2.3.4, ":port" if it isn't
                            18256), Square removes it, X joins. R sets the
                            server list: every public server on it shows up.
                            "locked" servers ask for their password.
  Wi-Fi uses the first connection in System Settings > Network Settings.


MULTIPLAYER > PROFILE
  Name: X opens the on-screen keyboard (empty = the PSP's nickname).
  Player model, Player colour (tints your model and name tag) and
  Physics gun colour: X or Right / Left to change. Circle to go back.


WORKSHOP (main menu)
  Turns maps, Lua addons and custom (Workshop) characters on and off.
  L / R: tab (Maps, Addons, Custom)   Up / Down: choose
  Left / Right: a page at a time      X: on / off
  Triangle: the whole tab on / off    Circle: back (saved)
  Maps are listed by game: X opens a game, Triangle switches all its maps.


ACHIEVEMENTS (main menu)
  D-pad Up / Down to scroll, Circle to go back. Shows what you've unlocked,
  progress on the counted ones, what you're playing on and your playtime.


MAIN MENU
  D-pad to choose, X to select, Circle to go back.


  The version number is in the bottom-left corner.
